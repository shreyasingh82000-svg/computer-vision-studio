## 🎯 What to Do Next

### Step 1: Upload an Image 📤
1. Click the **"📤 Upload Image"** button
2. Or press **Ctrl+O**
3. Select any JPG, PNG, BMP, or TIFF image from your computer

### Step 2: Explore the Tabs 🔍
Navigate through 6 operation categories:

#### **Tab 1: 🎨 Basic Operations**
- Grayscale conversion
- Histogram equalization
- Image inversion
- Brightness control (-100 to +100)
- Contrast control (0.5 to 2.0)

#### **Tab 2: 🔧 Filters**
- Gaussian Blur
- Median Filter
- Sharpen
- Bilateral Filter (edge-preserving)

#### **Tab 3: 📐 Edge Detection**
- Sobel Edge Detection
- Canny Edge Detection
- Laplacian Edge Detection

#### **Tab 4: 🎚️ Thresholding**
- Binary Threshold (with adjustable value)
- Adaptive Threshold

#### **Tab 5: 🔬 Morphological**
- Erosion
- Dilation
- Opening
- Closing

#### **Tab 6: 🔄 Transform**
- Rotate 90°, 180°, 270°
- Flip Horizontal
- Flip Vertical

### Step 3: Try These Features 🌟

**Undo/Redo:**
- Press **Ctrl+Z** to undo last operation
- Press **Ctrl+Y** to redo
- Up to 20 levels of history!

**View Histogram:**
- Click **"📊 Histogram"** button
- See interactive plot of pixel distribution
- Separate RGB channels visualization

**Image Information:**
- Click **"ℹ️ Image Info"** button
- See dimensions, channels, format, and history

**Tooltips:**
- Hover over ANY button
- See helpful descriptions

**Status Bar:**
- Watch the bottom of the window
- Real-time feedback on operations

## 🎯 Quick Demo Workflow

Try this 2-minute demo:

1. **Ctrl+O** → Upload a photo
2. Click **"🎨 Basic"** tab
3. Click **"⚫ Grayscale"** → See conversion
4. **Ctrl+Z** → Undo (back to color)
5. Move **Brightness** slider to +50
6. Click **"Apply Brightness"** → Brighter!
7. Click **"🔧 Filters"** tab
8. Click **"✨ Sharpen"** → Enhanced details!
9. Click **"📐 Edge"** tab

11. Click **"📊 Histogram"** → View distribution!
12. **Ctrl+S** → Save your result!

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### "Nothing happens when I click buttons"
**Solution:** Upload an image first! (Ctrl+O)

### "Where's the histogram?"
**Solution:** Click "📊 Histogram" button in toolbar

### "How do I undo?"
**Solution:** Press Ctrl+Z or use Edit → Undo

### "Sliders don't work"
**Solution:** Move slider, then click "Apply" button

### "Window too big/small"
**Solution:** Resize window manually (drag corners)
